## Tuning notes

Every number changed while tuning power parity (all in `lib/src/model.dart` unless noted):

| number | before | after | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| kPowerCost (charge cap) | 100 | 500 | the tuned costs sit far above the old cap |
| kZoomiesChargeCost | shared kPowerCost=100 | 460 | zoomies sends 2 obstacles per use, which suppresses BOTH players' scores in mirror matches; the highest cost throttles it until parity |
| kShadowPounceChargeCost | shared kPowerCost=100 | 500 | pounce clears ~13 pieces and refills its own charge; cost sits at the cap so it only fires on a full bar |
| kLaserChaseChargeCost | shared kPowerCost=100 | 175 | weakest direct effect; cheap so it is used at similar frequency |
| kLaserBreakCount | 6 | 8 | closes the per-use gap with the other powers |
| PowerBot fallback | all legal moves | swaps only (`lib/src/bots.dart`) | prevents cross-power contamination in single-power settings |
| harness execution | sequential | `Isolate.run` per setting (`tool/harness.dart`) | 2000 matches per setting in reasonable wall time |

Power effects themselves are contract-fixed (a row, the most common colour, N random pieces) and were not changed; parity is measured as mean match score per dedicated power setting, since per-use points cannot be equal by construction. Zoomies remains the weakest setting because its obstacle payload suppresses both sides symmetrically; its charge cost is the only remaining lever and is already near the practical ceiling.
